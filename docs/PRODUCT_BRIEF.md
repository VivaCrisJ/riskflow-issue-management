# RiskFlow — Product Brief

## Purpose

RiskFlow is a portfolio prototype demonstrating how structured data, workflow
automation and human-reviewed thematic analysis can support the issue-management
lifecycle in a UK financial-services risk environment.

All records are synthetic. The project contains no client, employer or personal
data.

## Target users

- Risk & Controls Analyst
- Controls Testing Team
- Head of Controls / Risk Services
- Business issue and remediation owners

## Core product promise

Turn fragmented risk and control issues into structured, actionable management
information.

## Primary interview walkthrough

1. Review the Monday-morning priority queue.
2. Open a high-severity overdue issue awaiting closure.
3. Assess remediation and closure evidence.
4. Request further evidence because design is documented but sustained operating
   effectiveness has not been demonstrated.
5. Show how the decision updates issue status, governance reporting and the
   management dashboard.
6. Finish with human-reviewed thematic classification of a new issue description.

## Issue lifecycle

1. Identified
2. Under Assessment
3. Action Plan Agreed
4. Remediation in Progress
5. Awaiting Closure Review
6. Further Evidence Required, where applicable
7. Closed
8. Reopened, where applicable

`Overdue` and `Escalation Required` are derived flags, not lifecycle statuses.

## Reporting basis

The initial demonstration uses a fixed reporting date of **30 September 2026**
so that ageing and overdue metrics remain stable during interviews.

## MVP modules

1. Executive Dashboard
2. Priority Review Queue
3. Issue Register with filtering
4. Issue Detail and Remediation Review
5. Closure Evidence Review
6. Thematic Analysis and assisted classification

## Responsible automation principle

Automated analysis supports professional judgment. It does not approve issue
closure, determine regulatory conclusions or replace accountable human review.
